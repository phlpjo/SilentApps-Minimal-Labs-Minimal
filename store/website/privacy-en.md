# Privacy – Minimal

**In short:** Minimal collects no personal data, has no servers and shares nothing with third parties. No account, no ads, no tracking, no analytics or crash SDKs.

## What is stored on your phone

- Favourite apps and folders, renames, icons, hidden apps and apps marked as distracting
- Gestures, font and display options

None of it leaves the device unless you share it yourself via the Android share menu. If Android device backup is turned on, Android backs up the app data like for any other app – you control that in the Android settings.

## Internet

Minimal has **no** internet permission and never connects to anything outside your phone.

## Permissions

| Permission | What for | Required? |
|---|---|---|
| List apps (`<queries>`) | so the app list can show your apps | yes |
| Accessibility service “Minimal – double-tap locks” | only to lock the screen on double-tap, reads no content | no, only after your consent |
| Usage access (`PACKAGE_USAGE_STATS`) | screen time, evaluated on the device only | no, only if you allow it |
| `EXPAND_STATUS_BAR` | swipe gesture for notifications | yes |
| `REQUEST_DELETE_PACKAGES` | “Uninstall” in the app menu – Android asks every time | yes |

## Deleting

Uninstalling the app or “Clear data” in the Android app settings removes everything completely.
