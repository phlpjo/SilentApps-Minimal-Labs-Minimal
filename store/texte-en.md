# Minimal – Store texts English (en-US)

## Title (max. 30)
Minimal – calm launcher

## Short description (max. 80)
A black-and-white home screen that slows down distraction. Offline, no ads.

## Full description (max. 4000)
Minimal makes your phone calmer. Instead of colourful icons you see the time, the date and your key apps as plain text. Everything else is one gesture away.

HOME SCREEN
• Time, date and your favourite apps as text
• Folders that show as a single word
• Optional battery and today's screen time
• Long press opens the settings

APP LIST
• Swipe up for all apps, sorted A to Z
• Search with one tap, letter bar on the side
• Rename, hide, pick a line icon, uninstall

SLOW DOWN DISTRACTION
Mark apps as distracting. Before they open, a short pause with a breathing circle appears – just enough time to notice whether you really want that app. You set the length.

GESTURES
• Swipe down: notifications (or an app of your choice)
• Swipe left or right: your choice
• Double-tap locks the screen (optional, after your consent)

CALM DESIGN
Black and white, light or dark following your system, easy-to-read type, a slow starfield in the background (can be turned off). Three fonts, three sizes.

YOUR DATA STAYS WITH YOU
• No internet permission
• No account, no ads, no tracking
• Screen time is calculated on the device only

ACCESSIBILITY SERVICE NOTICE
For “double-tap to lock”, Minimal uses the Android accessibility service API. It is used solely to lock the screen; Minimal reads no content with it. The feature is optional and only active after your explicit consent.

Buy once, stay calm.
